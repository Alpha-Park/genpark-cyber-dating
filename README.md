# genpark-cyber-dating

Digital twin & relationship wingman AI.

Stop paying for closed SaaS apps. This is a terminal-native, open-source alternative to **赛博蒸馏 (Cyber Distillation)**.

Upload your chat logs, and this skill builds your digital clone. It can draft dating app responses, analyze conversational chemistry, and 'distill' relationship formulas locally, keeping your romantic data strictly out of corporate servers.
